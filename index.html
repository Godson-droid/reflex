Reflex.

Reflex is a stop loss and take profit guardian that lives inside a Reactive smart contract. Set two prices and walk away. When the market crosses one, the contract sells the position itself. No keeper, no bot, no one watching a screen.

Then it was made to prove it: 64 complete executions on chain, each one a real origin swap, a real reaction on Lasna, and a real protective sale settled back on Base Sepolia.

The mechanism

Three chains of events, zero humans

A normal stop loss needs a keeper watching prices around the clock. Reflex moves the watching into the contract itself, across two networks.

01

The crossBase Sepolia · ReflexPair

A swap on the pair moves the WETH price through the band edge, 2,700 on the way down or 3,300 on the way up. The pair prints a Sync event with the new reserves. Nothing else has to happen. The market itself pulls the trigger.

event Sync(uint112, uint112) band 2,700 / 3,300 USDC per WETH
02

The reactionReactive Lasna · ReflexGuardian

The guardian is a Reactive smart contract subscribed to the pair. It prices the event on Lasna and fires its own Triggered transaction. Nobody polls an API, nobody holds a key, nobody signs. The watcher is code, and it does not sleep.

event Triggered(kind, price, originTxHash) cost ≈ 0.027 lREACT per reaction
03

The saleBase Sepolia · ReflexCallback

The callback contract receives the reaction back on Base Sepolia and sells the protected 10 WETH into the pair, settling USDC to the owner and recording the execution on chain. Median time from cross to settled sale across 64 loops: 8 blocks.

record Execution(kind, observed, executed, amountIn, amountOut) then both contracts re-arm and keep watching
